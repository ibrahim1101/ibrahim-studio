All original vertices, openings and room dimensions are preserved; interior baked finishes remain intact. The normal arrival and walk modes display the complete shell and roof; wall cuts are used only for the explicit dollhouse mode.

FORM presentation changes: each original furniture model is centered and uniformly scaled in a studio environment. Original meshes, textures, UVs and authored materials are retained. Camera viewpoints, orbit controls, light colours and the floor/contact disc are added. These are independent studies, not exact models of catalogue products.
